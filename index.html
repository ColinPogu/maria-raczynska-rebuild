About Maria

Hi, I'm Maria.

I am a self taught watercolor artist and teacher. Watercolor came into my life as a way to heal after losing my twin brother, and it slowly grew into the thing I now get to share with students all over the world.

I am also a mom, which is partly why I care so much about lessons that meet you where you are. Whether you have never held a brush or you have been painting for years, my job is to walk beside you and explain the how and the why, so your growth feels steady and kind, not rushed.

Painting should feel like a calm place to land. I would love to help you find yours.

Maria
